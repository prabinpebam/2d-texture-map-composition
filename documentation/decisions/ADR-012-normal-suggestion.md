# ADR-012: Normal Interpretation Suggestion

- Status: Disabled pending representative corpus
- Evidence: `documentation/evidence/m1/SPIKE-007-normal-heuristics.md`

Keep explicit normal interpretation, convention, and flip controls. The bounded
heuristic may remain test code but cannot suggest or mutate authored settings
until a representative corpus establishes confidence and false-positive rates.
Uncertainty always leaves the user-selected interpretation unchanged.
